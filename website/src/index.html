The town fixes its list of 101 eligible residents before collecting support. The demo has 102 registered accounts, including one for a child outside that list. The thresholds use all 101 eligible residents.

Who can take part?

Taking part requires an account and a place on the fixed list. Eligible residents who have no account still count towards the thresholds.

When does voting open?

At least one third of eligible residents must support holding a referendum. Someone who supports holding a vote can still vote against the proposal.

Check the support threshold

Count each supporter once. With 33 supporters, 3 × 33 = 99, below the required 101. With 34, 3 × 34 = 102 and voting opens.

What difference does one vote make?

Decision threshold: 68 of 101.

Adoption requires at least two thirds of all eligible residents to vote yes. Rejection requires the same number to vote no. Abstaining or staying home leaves the threshold unchanged.

Check the decision threshold

Twice the electorate is 202. A count of 67 gives 3 × 67 = 201. A count of 68 gives 3 × 68 = 204. The same test applies to no votes, so 34 yes and 67 no is also indeterminate.

What happens next?

After an indeterminate result, a repeat can be requested three calendar years after the scheduled close. A new procedure starts only when someone explicitly requests it.

The proposal keeps the same version. One third of the new electorate must support holding the vote again.
